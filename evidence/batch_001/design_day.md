## Method

GEV fit of each station's annual maximum daily HDD65 over complete heating years since 1995-96; 1-in-30 is the planning standard, 1-in-50 is shown alongside. Peak-day Dth = current customers x the model's use per customer at the design temperature, with a 90% regression forecast-error band. Contracts in `cgu_fpa.firm_capacity` are fictional.
