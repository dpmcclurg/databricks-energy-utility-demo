# Statewide backtest on real data (EIA Colorado)

Run: batch `001`, as of `2026-11-06`. Generated by `src/notebooks/08_evidence.py` from the tables.

## Method

Real EIA Colorado residential plus commercial gas volume (MMcf) per customer per day, regressed on the statewide customer-weighted HDD (NOAA stations weighted by `cgu_fpa.ref_weather_station.state_hdd_weight`). For each held-out winter the model is trained on the five gas years before it, then predicts November to March given the actual weather. 1 MMcf is about 1,037 Dth.
